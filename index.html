About Me

A little glimpse into who I am

Hello! I'm Ayan Biswas, a student from India who believes life is meant to be lived beyond the ordinary. Not just the same old routine — there's a whole world out there waiting to be explored.

I'm deeply passionate about traveling — exploring new places, meeting new souls, collecting moments that turn into stories. When I'm not on the road, you'll find me writing poetry that spills straight from the heart, or cooking something experimental in the kitchen.

Books are my escape. I love getting lost in pages and discovering perspectives I never knew existed. Every single day is a new chance to learn, to grow, to become a better person than I was yesterday.

At the end of the day, I just try to stay happy and cheerful — because life's too short for anything less. 😊
